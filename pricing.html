// billing_faq

Billing questions

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. You keep Pro benefits until the end of your billing period, and you won't be charged again.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, plus local payment methods in many countries. All payments are processed by a secure payment provider — we never store card details.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes — verified students get 40% off Hacker Pro. Contact us with your student email to apply.

What happens to my entry fee if an event is cancelled?

You get a full refund automatically, usually within 5–10 business days.

Are prices shown with tax?

Prices are shown in USD before any local taxes, which are calculated at checkout based on your country.
