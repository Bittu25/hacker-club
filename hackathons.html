How a Hacker Club hackathon works

01

Register & team up

Join solo or with a team. Use the team finder to meet people with complementary skills.

02

Kickoff

Themes and tracks go live. Watch the kickoff stream, then start building immediately.

03

Build with mentors

Mentors hold open office hours throughout. Ship updates to your project page as you go.

04

Demo & judging

Submit a repo, a 3‑minute demo video and a pitch. Judges score on impact, execution and creativity.
