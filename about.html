// our_story

Why we started Hacker Club

Great developers come from everywhere — but the best opportunities to show what you can do are still concentrated in a few places, time zones and networks.

We believe a well-designed competition is the fairest interview there is. The problem doesn't care where you studied or who you know. It only cares whether your code works.

So we're building a single, global arena: timed coding contests for algorithm lovers, themed online hackathons for builders, and real prize money for the people who win. Fair judging, instant feedback and payouts that work in every country.

// values

What we believe

01 /

Fair play, always

Transparent rules, sandboxed judging, plagiarism checks and human review of every winning entry. Trust is the product.

02 /

Global by default

Events across time zones, prices that make sense worldwide and payouts to 150+ countries.

03 /

Learn by competing

Every contest ends with editorials and replays. Losing a round should still make you better.

04 /

Reward the builders

Most of every entry fee goes straight into prize pools. When the community grows, the prizes grow.
